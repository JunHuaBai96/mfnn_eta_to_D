\opening{Dear Editors,}

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled
\textit{Residual multi-fidelity neural network correction of a Sutherland-type $\eta\to D$ map for Al-dominated liquid alloys}
for consideration in \textit{Journal of Computational Physics}.

Liquid diffusivities in multicomponent melts are scarce relative to viscosity, while classical Stokes--Einstein / Sutherland maps leave systematic residuals against experiment. We submit a residual multi-fidelity neural network (MFNN) that corrects a modified Sutherland $\eta\to D$ prior toward high-fidelity experimental diffusivities for Al-dominated liquid alloys. The residual formulation follows Lu et~al.\ (\textit{Proc.\ Natl.\ Acad.\ Sci.\ U.S.A.}\ 117, 7052, 2020) and extends the composite multi-fidelity network of Meng and Karniadakis (\textit{J.\ Comput.\ Phys.}\ 401, 109020, 2020). This work uses only that multi-fidelity regression idea; it is physics-guided and does \textbf{not} employ PDE-residual PINN training or Meng's parallel-in-time PPINN framework.

\textbf{Primary contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Physics-guided residual multi-fidelity learning for $\eta\to D$.}
Under within-composition temperature hold-out (Protocol~W; five seeds; $n_{\mathrm{test}}=15$), the residual MFNN attains MAPE $26.18\pm 1.33\%$, Factor2 $0.96\pm 0.033$, and $R^2=0.392\pm 0.094$, improving continuous accuracy and compressing seed-to-seed MAPE standard deviation by $\sim 70\%$ relative to HF-only (MAPE $30.98\pm 4.43\%$, Factor2 $0.973\pm 0.033$, $R^2=0.263\pm 0.076$), while far exceeding the LF-only network trained on Sutherland-generated $\log_{10} D_{\mathrm{LF}}$ targets (MAPE $109.6\pm 13.13\%$, $R^2=-2.588\pm 0.399$).
\item \textbf{Strict data provenance without synthetic leakage.}
We enforce pairing exclusively with measured or recommended viscosity correlations, explicitly excluding empirical Kozlov alloy viscosity fills that would otherwise introduce silent target leakage.
\item \textbf{Thermodynamic diagnosis of composition-transfer boundaries.}
Through modality-stratified leave-one-composition-out (LOGO) benchmarks within Al--Cu ($\mathrm{MAPE}=39.43\pm 39.71\%$), we isolate why interdiffusivity folds recover once diffusion modalities are encoded whereas tracer folds remain bounded by a $\sim 3\times$ thermodynamic scale offset (Darken factor) and neighborhood sparsity.
\end{enumerate}

All data tables, training scripts, and multi-seed evaluation protocols are provided to ensure full reproducibility.
